BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

Is the website price a monthly charge?

No. Starter, Business, and Premium are one-time build prices. Optional website care is monthly. Custom systems have their own setup and ongoing scope.

Do I need ongoing care?

You can discuss a build-only project or a build with care. Your proposal explains the hosting arrangement, ongoing responsibilities, and any recurring costs.

Are AI employees ready to run my business?

AI employee workflows require a separate implementation: connected tools, defined tasks, permissions, review steps, and usage costs. We scope and verify those connections before treating a workflow as operational.

Can I update my own website?

A customer portal and guided content editing can be included in the agreed scope. We define which text and images you can change, and how changes are reviewed and published.

Are advertising and software costs included?

Advertising spend, third-party subscriptions, payment processing, messaging, and AI usage are separate unless your proposal expressly includes them.

What happens after I inquire?

We review your goals and current tools, recommend a scope, and send a proposal with pricing, project steps, and responsibilities before work starts.
