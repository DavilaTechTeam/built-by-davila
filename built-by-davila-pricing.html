GOOD TO KNOW

Pricing questions, answered.

Do I have to pay everything up front?

No. For most projects, we can split the project into scheduled payments tied to the project timeline. The exact terms are included in your proposal.

Is hosting included?

Hosting and ongoing care are separate from the one-time website build unless your proposal specifically includes them.

Can I provide my own domain?

Yes. If you already own your domain, we can connect the completed site to it. If you need one, we can help you get it set up.

What if I need something not listed?

That’s normal. These packages are starting points. Custom integrations, e-commerce, portals, software and larger content needs are quoted separately.
